Market consequences IF THIS TREND UNFOLDS
  • Opportunity — manual-review firms shift from watching footage to verifying AI flags; more sites covered per reviewer
  • Opportunity — continuous, comparable fish-passage data creates a market for standardized reporting
  • Risk — consolidation around whichever platform becomes the de-facto standard; smaller operators locked into one vendor
  • Risk — false confidence in automation without human oversight, undercounting edge cases like night/floods
Desired future WHAT WE WANT
  • Every regulated fish-pass has continuous, low-cost, edge-deployed monitoring — adopted because it's cheaper and better, not mandated
  • Fish recall stays protected during floods, night, and peak migration instead of degrading invisibly
  • Monitoring data becomes an open, comparable evidence base for WFD reporting — not a closed platform
  • Ecologists spend time on verification and decisions, not staring at raw footage
